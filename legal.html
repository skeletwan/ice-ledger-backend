1.2 What Ice Ledger is

Ice Ledger is a hobby tool for hockey-card collectors. It lets you photograph cards, receive an automated identification guess, store a personal binder, enter what you paid, view estimated sold comps, share a public binder, follow other binders, like and comment, and (if offered) buy a paid plan (“Ice+”).

Ice Ledger is not a dealer, auction house, appraiser, broker, or financial adviser. Comp figures are estimates assembled from public sold listings and language-model search. They can be wrong, stale, or incomplete. Do not buy, sell, insure, or report taxes based only on Ice Ledger numbers.
